# Analysis review checklist

This file tracks the checks to complete before adding numerical findings to the public README.

- [ ] Confirm the minimum and maximum incident dates in the source extract.
- [ ] Check year coverage for 2012–2016 and determine how missing-date records affect the displayed views.
- [ ] Record annual totals and verify crime-category comparisons in the heatmap.
- [ ] Check the monthly and weekly grouping logic and the time periods being compared.
- [ ] Verify the categories and denominator used in the "Top 15 + Other" location-type grouping.
- [ ] Confirm the time range and other filters on the geographic worksheet.
- [ ] Confirm the arrest-status definition and the denominator for the percentages.
- [ ] Keep District 11 findings and Districts 8 and 11 comparisons separate from citywide findings.
- [ ] Export readable Tableau screenshots and review chart titles, filters, and labels.

## Known presentation limitation

The location-type treemap on Dashboard 02 has crowded labels in small tiles. Open its dedicated worksheet, **Chicago Crime by Location: Top 15 + Other (2012–2016)**, for a larger view. This is a display issue; the source data have not been changed to address it.

## Reporting approach

For each finding, report the observation, period, scope, and verified figure. Note meaningful limitations. Do not suggest that differences in reported incident counts establish causation.
